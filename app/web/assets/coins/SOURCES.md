# Cryptocurrency marks

All SVGs are served locally. The icons identify payment currencies and blockchain networks, without implying endorsement.

- SOL: official logomark from https://solana.com/branding — https://solana.com/src/img/branding/solanaLogoMark.svg. Geometry and gradient preserved.
- LTC: official “Coin Blue with White L” from https://litecoin.com/resources. Adobe metadata removed; geometry and colors preserved.
- TON: coin mark geometry extracted from the official vector logo at https://ton.org/media/ (https://ton.org/assets/media/ton_logo.zip), presented in the existing blue/white treatment. Wordmark excluded.
- BNB: official BNB Chain symbol from https://www.bnbchain.org/en/brand-guidelines — used to identify the BEP20 network. Geometry and yellow color preserved.
- BTC, ETH, USDT and TRX: existing recognizable currency marks retained from the supplied project. Tether's corporate logo was not substituted for the USDt currency mark.

Picker logos use consistent 32 px boxes (29 px on mobile), with object-fit: contain. Network selectors use 14 px marks. No external runtime requests or additional dependencies.
